September 2026 — Average Employee Tenure

Mean of (report date − hire date) across active employees, in years. Guardrail — hold at 2.2 yrs. Source: Marigold Home roster export (people table).

Average employee tenure is a stability guardrail rather than a traditional gap-to-goal driver. The current target is to hold average tenure at approximately 2.2 years.

There is no fixed annual dollar value assigned to this driver because the financial benefit is primarily associated with avoiding hiring, onboarding, and training costs. Those costs vary by department and employee role. The driver should therefore be viewed as a measure of retention and avoided replacement costs rather than a specific annual revenue or cash-savings figure.
