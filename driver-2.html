Decisions applied

Lead definition: any inbound contact expressing interest in an MHS service, including price-shoppers and existing customers asking about a different service or property. One lead per person per 30 days, matched on phone or email. A first-time radon test booking or mitigation estimate is the lead moment. Downstream progress shows up in Lead journeys only, so there's no double count with Driver 4 or Driver 9. Post-mit retests, fan repairs and system evaluations never count. For this mid-month build (decided Sept 23), the window is Mon Aug 31–Sun Sept 20, three complete weeks, and the headline is the 3-week average.

MHI referral exclusion: an MHI radon test (mitigation) or MHI order (RHM) at the same property address, dated before the first MHS contact, is a self-referral. It's excluded from the headline and tracked above. Matching is by address, never by name. "Zach Gustafson" shows up at many addresses as an MHI scheduling placeholder. A self-reported MHI connection isn't enough without an address match (Emily Ames is the standing example). Breeze tests placed by MHS's own technicians are MHS records, not MHI evidence.

RHM methodology & goal: RHM leads are first-time Routine Maintenance Quotes only, never a bare service visit (folded in Sept 19, 2026). The quote date is the Spectora order date. In Spectora exports an ordered $0 service shows as "0.0" and an unordered one as "0"; use that to identify quotes. ActiveCampaign is still pending. Goal and color scale (decided Sept 23): goal is 40/wk and baseline is 9/wk. The headline is green at or above goal, gold between baseline and goal, and red below baseline, based on distance to those lines, not month-over-month direction.
