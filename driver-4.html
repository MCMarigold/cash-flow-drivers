October 2026 — Follow-Up Close Rate

MHS — Mitigation only, October 1–31, 2026 · 70 real quotes sent · Source: Housecall Pro (live API, /estimates + /jobs, pulled Nov 2)

Follow-up close rate measures what share of MHS quotes sent turn into accepted jobs. From October this driver is Radon Mitigation only, sourced entirely from Housecall Pro: Routine Home Maintenance (RHM) is being wound down and is no longer part of the number, and Square is no longer a source, so there is no combined line anymore. The September page showed Combined, Mitigation and RHM; October's headline is the Mitigation rate.

This is a cohort measure: every real quote sent in October, judged as of November 2. 33 of the 70 quotes (47%) are still undecided, and most of those were sent in the last two weeks of the month, so the rate will move before the next report. Closing this month's sent quotes at the 70% target instead of the actual rate would have produced about $42,548 more (see Revenue at target below).

Formula

Close rate = quotes accepted ÷ quotes sent, for real (non-test) Mitigation quotes created October 1–31 (Central time), judged as of November 2. "Sent" excludes $0 quotes (never priced). Quotes are counted one per property address (see Decisions applied). An estimate counts as accepted if any option was approved or converted to a job (checked before the estimate's own status, so a "pro canceled" estimate with an approved option counts as won); lost if the estimate was canceled or every priced option expired or was declined; otherwise still resolving, which stays in the denominator.
