Definitions — decided

Decide — resolved

What counts as a lead: any inbound contact expressing interest counts as a new lead — including price-shoppers and existing customers asking about a different service. Broadest definition, chosen for consistency across sources over precision; revisit once real volume is visible.

Decide — resolved

Dedupe rule: one lead per person per 30 days, matched on phone and email, regardless of how many channels or times they reach out in that window.

Decide — resolved (interim)

Booked estimates as leads: not folded into the headline count. HCP-booked estimates are tracked as their own labeled line (see Headline above) so the number doesn't double-count with Driver 4 (follow-up close rate) and Driver 9 (sales KPIs), which already own the estimate stage. Revisit if pre-estimate lead volume proves too thin to be a meaningful weekly number on its own.
