August 2026 — Follow-Up Close Rate

MHS — Mitigation + RHM, August 2026 · 76 real quotes sent · Sources: Housecall Pro (Mitigation, live API + jobs export) + Square (Mitigation stragglers + RHM) + Spectora (order history)

Follow-up close rate measures what share of MHS quotes sent turn into accepted jobs, across both product lines: Radon Mitigation and Routine Home Maintenance (RHM, launched April 2026). Mitigation moved to Housecall Pro this year (fully ramped by 8/10–8/11); RHM has always run on Square, which is also where any Mitigation quotes that slipped through outside HCP during the cutover are still tracked.

Handyman estimates (143 historical Square rows) are excluded entirely — MHS discontinued handyman services, and no Handyman quote has been sent since May 2026.

Financially, this driver is about conversion on a pipeline that's already there: closing this month's real sent quotes at the 70% target instead of the actual rate would have produced about $18,187 more in August alone (see Revenue at target below) — almost entirely from Mitigation, since RHM's dollar volume is still too small to move the number much either way.

Formula

Close rate = quotes accepted ÷ quotes sent, for real (non-test, non-handyman) MHS quotes with a send date in August, judged as of today. Combined = Mitigation + RHM pooled together; each line is also reported separately since they're different sales motions on different systems. "Sent" excludes Drafts (never delivered) in both systems.

Decisions applied

Decided

Draft-status quotes don't count as "sent." Applies to both Mitigation and RHM — a Draft was never delivered to the customer, so it's excluded from the sent denominator and from the backlog count. Pending/Undelivered quotes did go out and count as sent, whether or not they were ever decided.
