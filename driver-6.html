August 2026 — Cross-Selling / Ancillary Attach Rate

MHI only, August 2026 · 485 in-scope inspections (63 excluded — see Scope below) · separate build from the CC ancillary attach rate skill

Cross-selling measures how often MHI inspections include at least one ancillary service. The goal is to increase the attach rate from 50% to 55%.

The estimated annual financial impact is approximately $72.8K in additional revenue. The model assumes 140 inspections per week and estimates that a 5-percentage-point increase in attach rate would generate approximately $1,400 in additional weekly revenue, or $72,800 annually. Bundled radon testing counts as an attached service. Prepaid, complimentary, and zero-fee inspections are included in the denominator — a client can still add a service to a pre-paid inspection the same as any other. Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, and CE courses are excluded, since there's no real home inspection to cross-sell onto.

Decisions applied

Decided

Scope — what counts as an inspection: all inspections stay in by default, including pre-paid/comp/zero-fee. Excluded entirely: Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, Insurance Inspection, Recuperative Care, and Annual Home Evaluation — none of these have a real home inspection to cross-sell onto. The CE Instruction booking is excluded too, since it's a class, not an inspection. Prelisting and 11-Month stay in scope (clients can still add ancillaries; 11-Month had real attach activity). Cancelled-inspection-but-paid-ancillary bookings stay in scope, since that ancillary revenue is real even though the residential inspection itself was cancelled. These same rules were applied to recalculate the Apr–Jun baseline (56.2%, n=1,593), replacing the original 49.0%/paid-only figure so August and baseline are now on equal footing.

Decided

Add-on definitions: 2nd Kitchen counts (a genuine cross-sell choice on larger homes with a basement or in-law suite), bundled radon always counts as attached, and "Pests in The Home - 1037520" is excluded as a mislabeled CE line item.

Resolved

Revenue is now computable for both periods. Manual Spectora exports (Inspections_Export-381084.csv for August, Inspections_Export-381270.csv for the baseline) carry a per-add-on price column the MCP pulls don't have. For each period, the MCP file's presence-based scope/attach calls stay authoritative; the wide file is only used to pull dollar amounts for those same confirmed Order IDs, rather than re-deriving scope from price columns — a naive price-only approach missed several $0/comp bookings in both periods (a comp sewer scope and free CE class in August; several comp inspections and add-ons in the baseline).
