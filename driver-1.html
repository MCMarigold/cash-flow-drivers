Inspector available capacity measures how much inspection capacity is actually available after accounting for scheduled work. The goal is to keep available capacity between 5% and 10%. At least 5% gives the team enough room to absorb demand without consistently operating over capacity, and more than 10% open slots is not sustainable revenue-wise.

The estimated annual financial impact is approximately $364K–$416K. This represents a potential annual revenue opportunity associated with adding enough inspector capacity to reach the 5% floor of the target range. The underlying model estimates about $7,008 in additional weekly revenue per additional inspector, annualized to $364,416. This is a revenue opportunity, not direct cash savings, and the total increases if more than one inspector is needed.

Assumes demand holds steady; a hire who also grows total bookings needs more slots to reach the same %.
