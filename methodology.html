Dashboard-Wide Financial Impact

Each driver's Est. Impact figure is the estimated annual financial impact of closing the gap to that driver's target. These figures represent potential revenue increases and/or cost savings — not guaranteed cash generated. Two of the nine drivers (8: Average Employee Tenure, 9: Sales KPIs Achieved) are deliberately not assigned a dollar figure, to avoid double-counting impact that's already captured in the other seven — so the totals below reflect those seven.

Some driver values are well-established calculations; others are estimates based on assumptions that will get more precise as the underlying data improves. See each driver's card below for its individual basis.

Driver 1 MHI

Inspector Available Capacity

Target5%
Est. Impact~$416K/yr

Formula: Weighted slots consumed ÷ available slots

  • Numerator: each booking's weight = Spectora duration ÷ team average duration (3.26 hrs = "1 slot")
  • Denominator: standard day = 2 slots, extended "long" day (8:30–7:00) = 3 slots, plus Saturday rotation adjustments (+2/month standard)
  • Adjustments: PTO docks the day's slot value (2 or 3) via calendar + Spectora cross-check; Mark Rahja and trainees under 100 lifetime inspections excluded from core team
  • Data sources: Spectora durations/events export, Inspector/Tech Schedule sheet, PTO calendar, Airtable Inspector Milestones table, Employee Directory "First Day" field
  • Financial impact: ~$364K–$416K/yr — a revenue opportunity (not direct cost savings), based on ~$7,008/wk in additional revenue per additional inspector ($7,008 × 52 = $364,416/yr per hire); closing the current gap is modeled as needing about 1.0 additional standard inspector hire
  • Owner: Blake

Caveat: assumes demand holds steady; a hire who also grows total bookings needs more slots to reach the same %.

Driver 2 MHS

MHS Marketing Leads

Target40/wk
Est. Impact~$409K/yr

Formula: unique new leads per week, deduped one-per-person-per-30-days, reported as a 4-week average.

  • Definitions: any inbound contact expressing interest counts, including price-shoppers; RHM lead = first-time "Routine Maintenance Quote"; a direct-to-estimate mitigation contact (no prior test) still counts; MHI self-referrals and discounted employee RHM accounts excluded
  • Data sources: email notifications (parsed manually), Housecall Pro (source of truth for mitigation), Spectora order history (system of record for RHM), MHI Tests Performed export (matched by address), HCP's native lead pipeline
  • Financial impact: ~$409K/yr in potential revenue, based on the expected revenue from increasing lead volume and converting those leads into booked MHS work; the page notes this opportunity grows further as downstream drivers (like follow-up close rate) improve, so it isn't a fixed independent figure
  • Owner: Minh

Open: MHI Spectora export only covers Apr–Aug 2026; paid-channel (LSA) tracking unconfirmed; ActiveCampaign intended as system of record but not yet configured; RHM conversion rate directional only (sample of 8 contacts).

Driver 3 OPS

Wrong People / Wrong Seats

Target90%
Est. Impact~$142K/yr

Formula: right-seat % = people passing all three GWC checks (Get it / Want it / Capacity) ÷ total people in filled seats; vacant seats excluded from both sides.

  • Calculation: scored population from accountability charts, segmented into revenue-producing and non-producing roles; a lower-output assumption is applied to wrong-seat employees; the wrong-seat rate is allocated proportionally to estimate revenue and labor-cost impact
  • Data sources: Accountability Chart (main + MHI + MHS detail), Airtable Employee directory, Spectora/MHS exports (cancelled orders excluded), Mission & Values doc (pending leadership approval)
  • Financial impact: ~$142K/yr at the 90% target — ~$92,452/yr from added revenue (a 20% output lift assumed per corrected seat) plus ~$49,920/yr from reduced labor cost

Driver 4 MHS

Follow-Up Close Rate

Target70%
Est. Impact~$100K/yr

Formula: close rate = quotes accepted ÷ quotes sent, restricted to real (non-test, non-handyman) MHS quotes, evaluated as of report date.

  • Data sources: Housecall Pro API /estimates endpoint (Mitigation), Square CSV export (Mitigation stragglers + RHM)

  • Financial impact: ~$100K/yr — hitting the 70% close-rate target on typical quote volume vs. actual close rate; note the live page also derives a month-specific gap by applying the target rate to that month's actual sent-quote value, which will run higher or lower than this figure depending on volume
  • Owner: Minh

Driver 6 MHI

Cross-Selling / Ancillary Attach Rate

Target55%
Est. Impact~$72.8K/yr

Formula: (inspections with 1+ add-on ÷ total in-scope inspections) × 100. Also tracked: avg add-ons/inspection, total add-on revenue, add-on % of revenue.

  • Scope: excludes Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, Insurance Inspection, Recuperative Care, Annual Home Evaluation, CE Instruction — none have a real inspection to cross-sell onto
  • Data sources: Spectora MCP export (attach detection), manual Spectora export (per-add-on revenue), reconciled by Order ID
  • Financial impact: ~$72.8K/yr — modeled as a 5-percentage-point increase in attach rate generating ~$1,400/wk in additional revenue ($1,400 × 52 = $72,800/yr); actual add-on revenue, per-inspection average, and % of total revenue are also tracked directly off the monthly reports
  • Owner: Maggie

Caveats: baseline period recalculated on identical rules for apples-to-apples comparison each month; condo/townhome consistently underperforms single-family; prelisting inspections run at zero attach.

Driver 7 OPS

AI Process Efficiencies

Target40 hrs/wk
Est. Impact~$62.4K/yr

Formula: weekly time savings = sum of "Est. weekly time savings" across all live AI-supported processes. Annual labor value = hrs/wk × 52 × $30/hr average wage.

  • Data source: manual register, logged as each process goes live (spreadsheet of AI-supported workflows with estimated weekly time savings)
  • Financial impact: ~$62.4K/yr at the 40 hrs/wk target (40 × 52 weeks × $30/hr average wage) — an annual labor value, not automatically realized cash savings; only becomes real savings if the freed time reduces actual labor cost or is redeployed to revenue-producing work
  • Owner: Britney

Caveats: this is annual labor value, not automatically realized cash savings — only real if freed time reduces actual labor cost or is redeployed to revenue work; $30/hr is a flat average wage that may shift to actual rates; no automated data source; placeholder examples still in the table awaiting real processes.

Driver 8 OPS

Average Employee Tenure

TargetHold 2.2 yrs
Est. ImpactNot quantified in $

Formula: tenure = report date − hire date, in years, per active person. A rehire's clock resets (only current active record counts). Overall = mean across MHI + MHS + Office Staff.

  • Data source: Marigold Home roster export (people table)
  • Financial impact: no fixed annual dollar value is assigned — the page notes the financial benefit is primarily about avoiding hiring, onboarding, and training costs from turnover, not a figure that's been dollarized

DecideTolerance band not yet confirmed (suggested 2.0–2.4 range); whether a tiered calculation would better reflect departmental differences.

Driver 9 MHS

Sales KPIs Achieved

Target100%
Est. ImpactNot quantified in $

Formula: for each of the 4 KPIs, each week: met if actual ≥ goal. Weekly score = KPIs met ÷ 4. Monthly headline = average of the four weekly scores.

  • KPIs: meetings set, sales calls/touchpoints, estimates sent, estimates won/booked
  • Data source: Marigold - EOS - Scorecard.xlsx (live, Drive) — Sales section
  • Financial impact: deliberately not assigned a dollar value — the page states these sales activities feed into other revenue drivers (lead generation, follow-up close rate), and giving this driver its own dollar figure could double-count impact already captured elsewhere
  • Owner: Ron
