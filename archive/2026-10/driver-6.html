October 2026 — Cross-Selling / Ancillary Attach Rate

MHI only, Oct 1–31, 2026 · 399 in-scope inspections (80 excluded — see Scope below) · separate build from the CC ancillary attach rate skill

Cross-selling measures how often MHI inspections include at least one ancillary service. The goal is an attach rate of 60% (raised from 55%).

The checklist's estimated annual impact is approximately $72.8K in additional revenue; it was sized for the earlier 50% to 55% goal (see Confirm below). The model assumes 140 inspections per week. Bundled radon testing counts as an attached service. Prepaid, complimentary, and zero-fee inspections are included in the denominator — a client can still add a service to a pre-paid inspection the same as any other. Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, and CE courses are excluded, since there's no real home inspection to cross-sell onto.

Decisions applied

Decided

Scope — what counts as an inspection: all inspections stay in by default, including pre-paid/comp/zero-fee. Excluded entirely: Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, Insurance Inspection, Recuperative Care, Annual Home Evaluation, Appraisal, Inspectify, CE Instruction, and (new, Nov 3) Dryer Vent Cleaning. Prelisting, 11-Month and Single Item stay in scope, as do cancelled-inspection bookings whose ancillary was still paid. Mixed bookings are scoped by their in-scope inspection. Goal is 60%; color tiers keep the same 5-point band below goal: green at 60% or more, gold from 55% to under 60%, red under 55% (recalculated from the old 55/50 tiers, Nov 3).

Decided

Add-on definitions: 2nd Kitchen counts, bundled radon always counts as attached, Whole Home Infrared and Pool are their own families, and "Intrusive Moisture (Stucco)" is now its own family (Nov 3: rare, but a service we offer and add on). CE-course line items ("name + numeric ID") are excluded. Toilet Pull/Roof Access (Nov 3) counts toward revenue, rolled into Sewer scope since it only happens on a sewer scope job, and never counts as an attach on its own. None were booked in October.

Decided

Revenue methodology: Spectora MCP scope and attach calls are authoritative (service and add-on names for all 479 October orders, cancelled included). The wide export supplies dollar amounts only, for those same Order IDs: all 479 matched 1:1, booking fees matched quotes on every order, and each order's fee reconciles to its service and add-on columns with no residual. The wide export repeats three column names (Radon (SF), Level 2 Chimney (SF), Mold Testing (SF)), so those columns are summed, not overwritten.
