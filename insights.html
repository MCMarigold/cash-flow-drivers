Big Picture

This Month at a Glance

This is the first Insights read against the dashboard, built from the August 2026 checkpoint (plus current live state for the three ops-style drivers) — so it's a baseline snapshot rather than a month-over-month trend; that comparison becomes possible once a second month is archived. Of the nine drivers, three are at or above target (Cross-Selling at 57.7% vs. 55%, Wrong People/Wrong Seats at 98% vs. 90%, and Average Employee Tenure at 2.56 yrs vs. a 2.2-yr hold), and one is essentially on the line (Inspector Efficiency, 79.9% vs. 80%). The other five are meaningfully below target, several by a wide margin.

Three items stand out as most worth time at the Sept 24 L10, in order: Sales KPIs Achieved is the widest gap on the board (18.75% vs. a 100% target) and has an open ownership question — two of its four KPIs are logged under Minh rather than Ron, which needs settling before anyone can be expected to move that number. AI Process Efficiencies is tracking only 2 of a targeted 40 hours/week saved, but since it's a manual register updated as each process goes live, it's worth confirming whether that reflects true progress or a logging gap. And Follow-Up Close Rate's RHM line (16.7%) is far enough below its Mitigation counterpart (42.9%) that the open question of whether this driver should carry one combined target or two separate ones is worth resolving now rather than later.

Several other drivers carry open "Decide" items on the methodology page (Wrong People/Wrong Seats' GWC scoring, Employee Tenure's tolerance band) that don't block this month's numbers but are worth a status check while the team is together.
