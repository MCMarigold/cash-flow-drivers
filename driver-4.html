Formula

Close rate = quotes accepted ÷ quotes sent, for real (non-test, non-handyman) MHS quotes with a send date in August, judged as of today. Combined = Mitigation + RHM pooled together; each line is also reported separately since they're different sales motions on different systems. "Sent" excludes Drafts (never delivered) in both systems.

Decisions applied

Decided

RHM added to Driver 4, sourced from Square. Mitigation now spans two sources for August: Housecall Pro (68 real sent quotes, the live system) plus 3 Square-sent Mitigation quotes from the cutover tail that never made it into HCP (Anne Pederson, Flynn & Mackenzie Rice, Jonathan Meidl — all Expired, real unanswered quotes, not duplicates). 3 other August Square Mitigation records were confirmed duplicates of HCP records already counted (Tim Parker, Bao Dang, Shante Lantos) and are excluded to avoid double-counting.

Decided

Handyman excluded entirely. MHS discontinued handyman services; 143 historical Square rows (all pre-May-2026) are dropped from scope rather than folded into either line.

Decided

Draft-status quotes don't count as "sent." Applies to both Mitigation and RHM — a Draft was never delivered to the customer, so it's excluded from the sent denominator and from the backlog count. Pending/Undelivered quotes did go out and count as sent, whether or not they were ever decided.

Decided

Formula stays single-stage (accepted ÷ sent), no "appointment held" step — this applies to RHM the same way it was decided for Mitigation: neither system tracks a separate booked-appointment stage that reliably precedes every quote.
