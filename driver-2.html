August 2026 — MHS Marketing Leads

Unique new leads per week, MHS only, reported as a 4-week average — three sources combined, RHM included

MHS marketing leads tracks the number of new leads coming into Marigold Home Services each week from marketing — inbound web/email inquiries and call-ins that book straight to a radon test, a mitigation estimate, or an RHM quote. Referrals from Marigold's own inspection arm (MHI) are tracked separately and excluded here, since they aren't marketing-generated. The goal is to increase the 4-week rolling average from 9 to 40 leads per week.

The estimated annual financial impact is approximately $409K in potential revenue. This is based on the expected revenue generated from increasing lead volume and converting those leads into booked MHS work. As other demand-side drivers, such as follow-up close rate, improve, the revenue opportunity from additional leads can increase as well.

RHM

What this is: August's 8 first-time RHM (routine home maintenance) contacts, traced through the MHS Spectora export the same way the mitigation leads above were traced through HCP/Spectora. Every one of them entered the same way — a first-time "Routine Maintenance Quote," Spectora's version of a mitigation estimate — so unlike the 3-starting-point mitigation funnel above, RHM has one starting point. Only a first-time quote counts as the lead moment — a "Routine Home Maintenance" or "Initial Routine Home Maintenance" service visit with no quote on record never counts, even when it's the earliest RHM row visible for that buyer, because a visit like that almost always means the actual quote happened before this export's window (or off-system) and just isn't in the data (see Data quality for the buyers this affects). Each lead was also address-matched against MHI's own Spectora order exports, the same rule used for the mitigation MHI referrals — 4 of the 8 turned out to be MHI referrals, an MHI home inspection at that exact address months before the person ever contacted MHS about RHM, and moved out of the counted total into their own row below. From the quote, each remaining lead was traced forward: did it turn into a first RHM visit, did that visit happen, and is there a next visit already on the books (RHM runs on a recurring ~quarterly cadence once a customer signs on, so a second future booking is the RHM equivalent of "installed").

Definitions — decided

  • What counts as a lead: any inbound contact expressing interest counts as a new lead — including price-shoppers and existing customers asking about a different service. Broadest definition, chosen for consistency across sources over precision; revisit once real volume is visible.
  • RHM leads are quotes only: a first-time "Routine Maintenance Quote" is the RHM lead moment. A "Routine Home Maintenance" or "Initial Routine Home Maintenance" service visit never counts as the lead by itself, even when it's the earliest RHM record visible for that buyer — a visit with no quote on file almost always means the actual quote happened before this export's window (or off-system), so counting the visit would misdate an older lead into whichever month the visit lands in.
  • Dedupe rule: one lead per person per 30 days, matched on phone and email, regardless of how many channels or times they reach out in that window.
  • Booked jobs as leads: a first-time customer's HCP "Radon Testing" job is counted as a lead here, but only as their point of first contact with Marigold — the count doesn't also add a separate "estimate" or "job" line for them elsewhere, so there's no double-count with Driver 4 (follow-up close rate) or Driver 9 (sales KPIs), which track what happens after a lead converts, not whether it existed. Where each lead landed in that downstream journey is shown in "Lead journeys" above, not folded into this page's headline lead count.
  • Direct-to-estimate leads: a new customer whose first-ever contact with Marigold is a mitigation estimate, not a radon test, still counts as a lead — most likely they were tested by someone else (e.g. during a home-purchase inspection) and called Marigold specifically for mitigation. Same first-contact logic as above, just a different entry point. Judged against HCP's own job records as the source of truth for mitigation, not Spectora — Spectora is just the online-intake echo of a job that should already exist in HCP.
  • MHI referrals don't count here: when the "someone else" who tested them (mitigation) or inspected the home before them (RHM) was Marigold's own inspection arm (MHI), that's not a marketing-driven lead — it's Marigold referring business to itself. Excluded from this page's headline and weekly counts even when it's the customer's first-ever MHS purchase; shown separately in Lead journeys for the full picture. Applies to both mitigation and RHM leads, matched by property address in both cases — MHI's Tests Performed export for mitigation, MHI's Spectora order exports for RHM.
  • Matching stays private: cross-referencing lead names against HCP/Spectora/MHI job records to build the Lead journeys section was done offline from your files. Only the resulting counts are published here — the matching itself, and any contact info used to do it, stayed off this public page.

Data sources

  • Email notifications: Marigold's email notifications for new Google leads and contact-us submissions, parsed manually/semi-manually. Doesn't require access to Minh's system, but loses whatever metrics his system calculates natively, and is more manual than a direct pull.
  • Housecall Pro job records — the source of truth for mitigation: used three ways — first, filtered to genuine first-time "Radon Testing" jobs to catch callers who booked a test directly (excludes Post-Mitigation Testing, valve/repair work, and system evaluations, which are downstream of an existing customer relationship, not a new lead); second, filtered to first-time mitigation jobs (quote through install) for people with no radon-testing job at all, since mitigation moved off Square to HCP this year; third, to check whether a lead's mitigation estimate turned into a completed install. Mitigation-side (MHI) only — RHM doesn't run through HCP.
  • Spectora order history: now mostly a duplicate of HCP for mitigation — customers submit a request in Spectora and it becomes an HCP job, so the same person usually shows up in both. Counted as its own mitigation lead only in the rare case where no matching HCP job exists at all (currently 2 people: Annie Slimmer, Stevan Pekich — see Flags). Spectora is also the system of record for RHM, where every first-time "Routine Maintenance Quote" counts as a Spectora-sourced lead — quotes only, never a bare service visit (see Definitions) — see Lead journeys — RHM.
  • MHI Tests Performed export (radon testing division): real-estate-transaction radon tests placed during MHI home inspections — a separate business line from MHS mitigation. Matched to Spectora/HCP mitigation records by property address (street + zip), never by name — the test is recorded under whoever's on file at the time, often the buyer, while the eventual mitigation customer is the seller calling in under a different name. "High" is ≥4.0 pCi/L, the EPA residential action level. Where a high MHI test leads to a mitigation estimate, that's a real referral, but it's Marigold's own inspection arm feeding its own mitigation arm — tracked in Lead journeys for visibility, excluded from this page's counts (see Definitions).
  • MHI Spectora order exports: two general MHI booking exports covering Order Dates April–August 2026 — every MHI order (home inspection, radon, sewer scope, etc.), not radon-specific like the Tests Performed export above, and with no buyer-name column at all, only address. Used to address-match August's RHM leads against a prior MHI order at the same property — the RHM equivalent of the radon-test referral check. Only covers Apr–Aug 2026, so an MHI order before April wouldn't be caught (see Flags).
  • Target: Minh's system is ActiveCampaign, tracking site form submissions — the intended system of record for this driver once access is set up. Closest to source, includes his existing calculated fields.
  • HCP's native lead pipeline (leads_report.csv): tracks phone-in callers who haven't yet become a job. Can likely be folded in once de-duped against the email and job-based leads already counted (see Flags).
  • Paid channels (LSA, etc.): platform-reported leads — to be added once channel status is confirmed (see Flags).
