This driver does not have a separate dollar value assigned to it. The sales activities measured here feed into other revenue drivers, including lead generation and follow-up close rate. Assigning an additional dollar value to this driver could double-count financial impact that is already captured elsewhere.

October is scored against the Scorecard's current goals (5 meetings, 125 sales calls, 16 estimates sent, 10 estimates won). September was published against the earlier 115 / 12 goals, so the two months are not directly comparable.
