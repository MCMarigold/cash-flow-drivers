Sep 1–20, 2026 — Cross-Selling / Ancillary Attach Rate

MHI only, Sep 1–20, 2026 (month to date) · 289 in-scope inspections (51 excluded — see Scope below) · separate build from the CC ancillary attach rate skill

Cross-selling measures how often MHI inspections include at least one ancillary service. The goal is to increase the attach rate from 50% to 55%.

The estimated annual financial impact is approximately $72.8K in additional revenue. The model assumes 140 inspections per week and estimates that a 5-percentage-point increase in attach rate would generate approximately $1,400 in additional weekly revenue, or $72,800 annually. Bundled radon testing counts as an attached service. Prepaid, complimentary, and zero-fee inspections are included in the denominator — a client can still add a service to a pre-paid inspection the same as any other. Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, and CE courses are excluded, since there's no real home inspection to cross-sell onto.

Decisions applied

Decided

Scope — what counts as an inspection: all inspections stay in by default, including pre-paid/comp/zero-fee. Excluded entirely: Phase 2, TISH, Re-Inspection, Walk & Talk, standalone add-on-only, Preventative Maintenance, Insurance Inspection, Recuperative Care, Annual Home Evaluation and CE Instruction. New this period, also excluded: Appraisal Inspection and Inspectify (third-party platform bookings). Prelisting, 11-Month and Single Item Inspection stay in scope; mold testing is regularly upsold on Single Item visits. A booking that pairs a Re-Inspection or a standalone service with an in-scope inspection is scoped by that inspection. Cancelled-inspection-but-paid-ancillary bookings stay in scope as "Other". Rows with no services and no add-ons are excluded as a data gap.

Decided

Add-on definitions: 2nd Kitchen counts, bundled radon always counts as attached, and CE-course line items ("name + numeric ID", e.g. "Home Inspections - The Essentials 1036948") and access fees ("Toilet Pull/Roof Access") are excluded. New this period: Whole Home Infrared is its own family. A "(Without Home Inspection)" service booked alongside an in-scope inspection counts as an attach to its family, even though Spectora records it as a service rather than an add-on. This period that's a Pool Inspection on a single-family job and Mold Testing on a Single Item Inspection.

Decided

Revenue methodology: the MCP export's presence-based scope and attach calls are authoritative. The manual wide export only supplies dollar amounts for those same confirmed Order IDs. All 340 Order IDs matched 1:1, and quotes matched booking fees on every order. The reconciliation diff turned up 3 $0 comp add-ons that a price-only check would have missed; they're counted as attached. Service-booked attaches (pool, mold on a Single Item) take revenue from that service's own price column; those orders always show up in the diff and are expected. Family average fees are averaged over bookings that attached, so comps pull them down as expected.
