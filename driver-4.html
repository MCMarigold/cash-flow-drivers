Formula

Close rate = quotes accepted ÷ quotes sent, for real (non-test, non-handyman) MHS quotes with a send date September 1–20 (Central time), judged as of September 22. Combined = Mitigation + RHM pooled together; each line is also reported separately since they're different sales motions on different systems. "Sent" excludes Drafts (never delivered) in both systems and $0 quotes (never priced, so never sent). In HCP, an estimate counts as accepted if any option was approved or converted to a job; lost if the estimate was canceled or every option expired/declined; otherwise still resolving.

Decisions applied

Decided

Draft-status quotes don't count as "sent." Applies to both Mitigation and RHM — a Draft was never delivered to the customer, so it's excluded from the sent denominator and from the backlog count. Pending/Undelivered quotes did go out and count as sent, whether or not they were ever decided.

Decided

Cohort-based, pending stays in the denominator. All real quotes sent in the period, judged as of run date. Decided-in-period-only overstates the rate (tested in August: 75.0% vs. 45.6%), and this period shows why — decided-only would read 89%.

Decided

$0 quotes don't count as "sent" (Maggie, Sept 22). A quote with no price was never actually sent to the customer. Like a Draft, it's excluded from the sent denominator and from every count, and it's listed in the flagged table so nothing disappears silently. This period that's 5 records (4 upcoming site visits and 1 canceled visit), so Mitigation reads 50.0% (17 ÷ 34) instead of 43.6% (17 ÷ 39). August's re-cut in the backlog section uses the same rule.
