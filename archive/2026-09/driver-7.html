September 2026 — AI Process Efficiencies

Live log · as of September 22, 2026 · 17 live processes carrying hours (5 proposed)

AI Process Efficiencies tracks staff time freed by live AI-supported workflows. There are two goals: 40 hours a week saved across the team, worth about $62.4K a year at a flat $30/hr average wage, and 15 live processes. Each row below is one process in the AI Processes log, and only live, repeating work counts toward the hours.

This is a record of what has been built so far, not a claim of having arrived. The dollar figure is annual labor value, not realized cash. It becomes a cost saving only if the freed time reduces labor costs, or revenue only if that time moves to revenue-producing work.

Formula

Weekly hours saved = sum of Est. Weekly Hours Saved across Live rows. Periodic work enters as (hours per run × runs per year) ÷ 52; one-time and new-capability rows carry 0. Annual labor value = weekly hours × 52 × $30/hr. Process count = Live rows whose Savings Type is Recurring or Periodic.

Decisions applied

Data entry & conventions

The AI Processes table in the dashboard's Airtable base is the system of record. This page is rebuilt from it, not edited by hand. Each row carries a Savings Type (Recurring, Periodic, One-time, New capability), a Basis (Measured, Estimated, Rough), and a Source so it can be re-verified. Proposed rows stay out of the total until they are in routine use.

Public page privacy

Rows marked Internal only never appear here by name or description. Internal rows that carry hours still count in the totals and show as one anonymous "withheld" line. Internal rows with no hours are not shown or counted on this page at all. This holds until the dashboard moves behind the intranet login.

Open items

Decide

Wage costing. $30/hr is applied as a flat rate to every process. With 17 processes logged, it's worth deciding whether to keep one blended rate or cost each process at the actual rate of whoever's time was freed.

Decide

How solid is solid enough. 3.3 of the 6.4 hours are measured; the rest are estimates or rough guesses. The checklist calls this the most gameable driver on the dashboard. Decide whether Rough rows should need a before-and-after timing before they count.

Confirm

Two numbers flagged to challenge first. The Radon Daily Fleet Job's 0.8 hrs/wk hasn't been rate-checked yet, and it's the least certain figure in the log. The Social Media Stats Pipeline's 0.5 hrs/wk was being counted while the pipeline was silently down from Jul 30 to Sep 17. It's fixed now, but its platform access token expires Sep 27.
