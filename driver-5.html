Tech booked hours

Tech appointments are often windows, so booked hours use a standard onsite time per service instead of the calendar block or Spectora duration, and add up when a stop has several services. Times (from Maggie): radon drop-off or pick-up 15 min (a bit generous; real is closer to 5), sewer scope 40, mold air samples 40, mold swab 20, well yield 120, well water analysis or testing and water test 15, chimney 40 plus about 40 per extra flue (two flues 90), lead paint 15, asbestos 15, pool 120. Inspector booked hours still come from Spectora booking durations, so the two groups are not measured the same way.
