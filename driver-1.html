Inspector available capacity measures how much inspection capacity is actually available after accounting for scheduled work. The goal is to move from 0% to 5% available capacity so the team has enough room to absorb demand without consistently operating over capacity.

The estimated annual financial impact is approximately $364K–$416K. This represents a potential annual revenue opportunity associated with adding enough inspector capacity to reach the 5% availability target. The underlying model estimates about $7,008 in additional weekly revenue per additional inspector, annualized to $364,416. This is a revenue opportunity, not direct cash savings, and the total increases if more than one inspector is needed.

Methodology

  • Numerator (weighted): each booking's weight = its Spectora duration ÷ the team's average duration (3.26 hrs = "1 slot"). TISH/Brett Fredrichs excluded (technician, not inspector). Multi-inspector bookings count full duration for both people.
  • Denominator (slots): standard day = 2 slots, extended "long" day (8:30–7:00) = 3 slots.
  • Long-day assignments: Aaron Vandeventer (Mon), Mitchell Bowser/Matt Clarity/Joel Terry (Tue), Kelly Brill (Thu), Stephen Hines (Mon+Thu, alternating weeks).
  • Saturday rotation: +2 slots/month unless starred (flex, no bonus). Starred: Mitchell Bowser, Joel Terry, Anthony Link-Danko, Kyle Kanipes. Matt Clarity gets +4 — confirmed he works both his regular Week 4 Saturday and the August 5th-Saturday.
  • PTO: docks the actual day's slot value (2 or 3), from the calendar export plus every confirmed block from the Spectora events export cross-check.
  • Mark Rahja: 1 slot/day, only when needed — excluded from core team. Dan Kosel: 9 slots/week (no Monday morning) — included.
  • New hires (Joel Terry, Travis East): solo-started Aug 14 (confirmed via Employee Directory "First Day" field). Excluded from the core team until each clears 100 lifetime inspections (Airtable Inspector Milestones) — currently at 39 and 47 respectively.
  • Open-slot review: every gap gets cross-checked against Spectora's own events export (full-month, categorized) before falling back to manual confirmation.

Hiring impact

Per Curtis's original meeting notes: "Each additional inspector brings approximately $7,008 a week." This is the revenue base behind the checklist's $364–416K driver impact — $7,008 × 52 weeks = $364,416 for one hire. Confirmed: the dollar figure was never "5% of a separate MHI/MHS revenue pool," it's the direct value of hiring enough inspectors to close the availability gap.

Holding demand (weighted consumption, 489.5) constant, the team needs 515.3 total slots to reach 5% available — a gap of 43.3 slots from the current 472. A standard inspector (no long day, no Saturday) contributes roughly 42 slots/month, so closing the gap takes about 1.0 additional standard hire — essentially one person, worth roughly the $364K figure already in the checklist. Assumes demand holds steady; a hire who also grows total bookings needs more slots to reach the same %.
