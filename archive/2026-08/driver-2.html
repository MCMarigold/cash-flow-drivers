August 2026 — MHS Marketing Leads

Unique new leads per week, MHS only, reported as a 4-week average — three sources combined, RHM included

MHS marketing leads tracks the number of new leads coming into Marigold Home Services each week from marketing — inbound web/email inquiries and call-ins that book straight to a radon test, a mitigation estimate, or an RHM quote. Referrals from Marigold's own inspection arm (MHI) are tracked separately and excluded here, since they aren't marketing-generated. The goal is to increase the 4-week rolling average from 9 to 40 leads per week.

The estimated annual financial impact is approximately $409K in potential revenue. This is based on the expected revenue generated from increasing lead volume and converting those leads into booked MHS work. As other demand-side drivers, such as follow-up close rate, improve, the revenue opportunity from additional leads can increase as well.

Definitions — decided

  • What counts as a lead: any inbound contact expressing interest — including price-shoppers and existing customers asking about a different service. Broadest definition, chosen for consistency over precision; revisit once real volume is visible.
  • RHM leads are quotes only: a first-time "Routine Maintenance Quote" is the RHM lead moment — a bare service visit never counts by itself, even as the earliest RHM record on file, since it usually means the real quote happened earlier (off-system or before this export's window).
  • Dedupe rule: one lead per person per 30 days, matched on phone and email, regardless of how many channels or times they reach out in that window.
  • Booked jobs as leads: a first-time customer's HCP "Radon Testing" job counts as their lead moment only — not also as a separate estimate/job elsewhere, so there's no double-count with Driver 4 (follow-up close rate) or Driver 9 (sales KPIs). Downstream progress is shown in Lead journeys, not folded into the headline count.
  • Direct-to-estimate leads: a new customer whose first contact is a mitigation estimate, not a radon test, still counts — most likely tested elsewhere and called specifically for mitigation. Judged against HCP's job records, not Spectora. Includes the rare lead caught only via HCP's native pipeline.
  • Employee RHM accounts don't count: employees get a discounted RHM rate, so those accounts aren't marketing leads — matched against the current roster and re-checked monthly (see Data quality).
  • MHI referrals don't count here: when the prior tester (mitigation) or inspector (RHM) was Marigold's own inspection arm (MHI), that's self-referral, not a marketing lead. Excluded from headline and weekly counts even on a customer's first MHS purchase; shown separately in Lead journeys. Matched by property address for both mitigation and RHM.
  • Matching stays private: cross-referencing lead names against HCP/Spectora/MHI records to build Lead journeys was done offline. Only the resulting counts are published here — the matching and any contact info used stayed off this page.

Data sources

  • Email notifications: Marigold's email notifications for new Google leads and contact-us submissions, parsed manually. No system access needed, but more manual and loses Minh's native metrics.
  • Housecall Pro job records — the source of truth for mitigation: catches direct test bookings, first-time mitigation jobs (quote through install) for people with no radon-testing job, and whether an estimate converted to an install. Mitigation only — RHM doesn't run through HCP.
  • Spectora order history: mostly duplicates HCP for mitigation — counted as its own lead only when no matching HCP job exists (currently 2 people: Annie Slimmer, Stevan Pekich — see Flags). Also the system of record for RHM, where a first-time quote counts as the lead, never a bare service visit (see Definitions).
  • MHI Tests Performed export (Breeze): real-estate-transaction radon tests from MHI home inspections. Matched to mitigation records by property address, never by name — buyer and mitigation customer are often listed under different names. "High" is ≥4.0 pCi/L (EPA action level). A high test leading to an estimate is a real referral, but it's MHI feeding its own arm — tracked in Lead journeys, excluded from this page's counts (see Definitions).
  • MHI Spectora order exports: two MHI booking exports, Order Dates April–August 2026, address only (no buyer name). Used to check RHM leads against a prior MHI order at the same address. Doesn't cover before April (see Flags).
  • HCP's native lead pipeline (leads report): HCP's own lead-tracking view, separate from job records — caught Jennifer Bjorklund's referral lead, which had no job or Spectora record. Only the lead-level export (name, tags, status, dates) supports de-duping; checked manually each month.
  • Paid channels (LSA, etc.): platform-reported leads — to be added once channel status is confirmed (see Flags).
